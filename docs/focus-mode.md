# Focused reading mode

Hides the tab bar, the reader's annotation toolbar, the reader sidebar and the item pane, and enters fullscreen.

- **Toggle:** F11 on Windows and Linux, Ctrl+Cmd+F on macOS, **Tools > RefWright > Toggle Focused Reading Mode**, or right-click in the PDF.
- **Leaving:** toggle again, or press Esc to leave fullscreen. If the window was already fullscreen before, it stays fullscreen.
- **Restored on exit:** the item pane and any reader sidebars that were open.
- **Settings > Focused reading:** turn off the shortcut, or keep the window out of fullscreen.

Readers opened while focused mode is on are hidden the same way.
