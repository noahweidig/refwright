# Bookmarks

Zotero's PDF reader can open a PDF's own outline but cannot add bookmarks. RefWright adds page bookmarks that live in Zotero, not in the file.

## Add a bookmark

- Click the **bookmark button** in the reader toolbar, or right-click the page and choose **Bookmark This Page**.
- Bookmarking a page that already has one does nothing and says so.

Each bookmark is a blue note annotation in the page's left margin with the comment *Bookmark* and the tag `refwright:bookmark`. You can edit the comment in the reader's sidebar, for example to write what is on the page, and delete a bookmark like any annotation.

## Move between bookmarks

Right-click in the reader and choose **Next Bookmark** or **Previous Bookmark**. Both wrap around. In the item pane, the RefWright section lists an item's bookmarks as `p. 12` buttons that open the PDF at that page. **RefWright > Open at Last Bookmark** in the item menu opens the one you added most recently.

## Sync

Bookmarks are annotations, so they sync with Zotero's data sync to your other computers. They are not written into the PDF, so other PDF viewers do not show them. Zotero's mobile apps show them as note annotations, but the RefWright buttons exist only on the desktop. A library you can't edit, such as a read-only group, can't hold bookmarks.

## Limits

- The item pane list updates when you select the item again, not the moment you add a bookmark.
- Bookmarks work in PDFs only, not in EPUBs or snapshots.
- Readers that were already open when you installed or updated RefWright get the button after you reopen them.
